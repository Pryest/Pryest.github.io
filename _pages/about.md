Hi! I am a first-year CS PhD student at NYU Courant (Shanghai Track), co-advised by Professor [Chen Zhao](https://henryzhao5852.github.io) and Professor [Yucheng Lu](https://www.yucheng-lu.me). Prior to that, I earned my M.S and B.S. degree in Computer Science from Fudan University, advised by [Xipeng Qiu](https://xpqiu.github.io/en.html). My research interests lie in natural language processing and large language models (LLMs).

Recently, I’ve been thinking about the following questions:
1. How can insights from model interpretability guide the design of better LLM architectures?
2. How can we improve the memory capabilities of LLMs?
3. Why is next-token prediction so effective, and how can we improve upon this paradigm?

If you have any thoughts on these, shoot me an email and I am more than happy to chat with you!
